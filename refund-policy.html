Refund Policy

Draft for review, 19 October 2026. Operated by WordPressistic LLC, doing business as WPistic.

Bridgistic Pro is billed through Paddle and licensed by WPistic. The WPistic Refund Policy applies to the monthly and yearly plans. This page sets out how it applies to Bridgistic.

1. Monthly plan (US$12.00 per month)

As the WPistic Refund Policy says, you can cancel at any time and keep access until the end of the period you have paid for. Prorated refunds for the unused part of a month are generally not issued, except where the law requires it or the exceptions in that policy apply.

2. Yearly plan (US$99.00 per year)

As the WPistic Refund Policy says, if you cancel within the first 30 days of a new annual billing cycle, you get a full refund for that cycle. After 30 days the year is non-refundable, but you can turn off renewal at any time.

3. Lifetime Deal (US$29.99, one payment)

The WPistic Refund Policy does not cover one-time purchases. Refund terms for the Lifetime Deal: Pending Shuvo review

4. How to ask for a refund

The WPistic Refund Policy asks you to email support@wpistic.com with your account email and the reason, and aims to reply within 2 business days. For Bridgistic you can also write to hello@insightistic.com. Pending Shuvo review

Approved refunds are paid to your original payment method. The WPistic policy says they usually appear within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

5. Exceptions

The exceptions in section 6 of the WPistic Refund Policy apply. They cover abuse, fraud, chargebacks filed in bad faith and violations of the acceptable use rules.
